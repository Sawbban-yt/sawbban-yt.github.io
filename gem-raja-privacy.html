Gem Raja — Privacy Policy

Developer: Sawbban Hasan · Effective date: 24 October 2026

1. Overview

Gem Raja is a fully offline match-3 puzzle game. It works without an internet connection and does not require any account, registration or sign-in.

2. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. This version of the game contains no analytics tools, no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking of any kind.

3. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress only on your own device, in the device's local storage, so the game can work offline. This includes:

  • your coins, boosters and backdrop themes;
  • the stars you have earned and the levels you have unlocked;
  • Daily Challenge streak and daily mission status;
  • settings, such as your sound preference, and your in-progress game.

This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the game.

4. Permissions

This version of the game requests no device permissions.

5. Children's privacy

Gem Raja is a general-audience game suitable for players of all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children either.

6. Third parties

This version of the game contains no third-party SDKs or services. If a future version adds such services (for example, advertising), this policy will be updated and the new version of the policy will be published on this page and inside the app before that version is released.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page and inside the app, with its effective date shown at the top.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the developer email address shown on the Gem Raja page on Google Play.
